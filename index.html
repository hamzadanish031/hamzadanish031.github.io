About

Beyond executing test cases

✓ ISTQB Certified
CTFL v4 · Karachi, PK

I'm an SQA Engineer with 4+ years of experience owning quality for web-based products and revenue-impacting experiments. My work isn't limited to running predefined checks — I take QA end-to-end, from understanding requirements and reviewing builds, through test planning and execution, defect management, regression, and release validation.

At Spiralyze, a conversion-optimization agency, I act as the final quality checkpoint for A/B test variants built for the live websites of major NASDAQ- and NYSE-listed companies. A broken variant means lost conversions and brand risk at scale — so every experiment is validated functionally, pixel-perfect against Figma, and across a full browser and device matrix before it goes live.

I work directly with developers, product stakeholders, and clients rather than in QA isolation — clarifying requirements, communicating risk, and reporting quality status. The direction I'm growing toward is senior QA ownership: strategy, process, and automation.

QA lifecycle

How I take a build from spec to safe release

I own quality across the whole delivery lifecycle — not a single hand-off stage.

01

Requirement review

Understand the spec, acceptance criteria, and the experiment's intent before anything is built.

02

QA planning

Define scope, test cases, scenarios, and the browser/device matrix for the change.

03

Build validation

Check the built variant against design and requirements — pixel-perfect vs Figma.

04

Functional testing

Verify every interactive element behaves correctly across the target environments.

05

API / integration

Validate endpoints with Postman and data integrity with SQL where the change touches them.

06

Defect management

Log, prioritize, and track issues to closure in Jira / Azure DevOps / Asana; re-verify fixes.

07

Regression

Confirm the fix or feature didn't break anything already working before sign-off.

08

Release validation

Final pre-launch gate and post-release verification, with quality status reported to stakeholders.
